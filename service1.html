Liquidation Services

At BDS Luxembourg SARL, we specialize in providing comprehensive liquidation services for regulated and non-regulated entities in Luxembourg. Our team is dedicated to guiding you through each step of the liquidation process, ensuring a smooth and efficient experience.



WHY CHOOSE US?



Focus on Your High-Performing Strategic Operations

Streamline your business by directing your attention to your most valuable entities. Our specialized team will manage the systematic closure of your inactive structures, enabling you to channel your efforts towards strategic growth and active business segments.

Proactive Deadline Management

Missing a single regulatory timeline could extend your administrative obligations and financial liabilities by an entire year. We meticulously track and meet all your compliance deadlines.

Optimized Business Wind-Down Strategy

Voluntary dissolution represents a scenario where proper procedures are executed in a systematic manner. We will ensure that the winding up of your business is conducted efficiently, methodically, and in full adherence to regulatory requirements.

Fixed Fees

Experience complete transparency with no hidden costs—our process is designed to provide clear, upfront pricing and full visibility at every stage, so you can proceed with confidence and peace of mind.
